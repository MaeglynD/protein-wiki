Molecular coordinates and experimental metadata: Worldwide Protein Data Bank, accessed through RCSB PDB. PDB archive coordinates: CC0.
https://www.rcsb.org/pages/policies
Structure-specific records, experimental methods and primary citations are linked from every molecule.
Curated metadata and coordinates retrieved 9 October 2026. Live archive search uses current RCSB Search and Data APIs.

Rendering: Mol* 5.13.1, MIT licence.
https://molstar.org/
Sehnal et al. (2021), Mol* Viewer: modern web app for 3D visualization and analysis of large biomolecular structures, Nucleic Acids Research 49(W1), W431–W437.
